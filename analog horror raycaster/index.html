How it works

The halls are a maze. The way out is the farthest cell; reach it and the tape descends one deeper — bigger, faster, less patient.

  • Two ways out. The green glow is the exit. The red glow is the den — where it beds down, and the other ending. That one pays triple, and you have to walk in.
  • Tapes lie in the corridors. Walk over one for shards.
  • Shards buy kit at the exchange (B). Kit survives death; depth does not.
  • Dread climbs the longer you stay. It thickens the fog, sours the picture and quickens the presence.
  • Hide — hold C while standing still. Dread falls and it loses your pace; step out and it catches back up.
  • It hunts, then goes home to its den and lies still, then comes back. Look at it from far off and it slips away — so you rarely see it whole. A ward survives one touch and sends it home.
  • Chart on M swings the minimap between close and wide. It only shows what you have already walked past.
  • Sprint on Shift burns breath; stop and it returns.
